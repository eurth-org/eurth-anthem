## How to review

- **Lines** are numbered (L1, L2, ...). Each shows the line as written, with its slots in square brackets, and one example filled in. Say which to cut or change: "cut L14", "L22: say *keep* instead of *hold*".
- **Phrases** are the words that fill the slots, in groups that can stand in for each other. Say which don't belong: "cut *hardy* from virtue".
- The **sample verses** at the end show how lines and phrases combine. If a combination reads wrongly, tell me which, and I'll find the line or the phrase behind it.
- Every line already scans and every rhyme is a true rhyme: the tests check that against the dictionary. What needs your eye is sense, tone and taste.
